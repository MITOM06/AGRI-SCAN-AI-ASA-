# Business Model & Development Roadmap — AGRI-SCAN-AI

## 1. Business Model

Even at the technology-focused MVP stage, the project lays out a clear path to profitability to ensure sustainability:

1. **Freemium Model:**
   - *Free:* Limited number of AI scans per day, access to the basic encyclopedia.
   - *Premium (monthly/yearly subscription):* Unlimited AI scans and chat, lifetime history storage, unlocking the in-depth Care Roadmap, and direct chat with agriculture experts.
2. **B2B Affiliate & Partnership (future):**
   - Partner with agricultural-supply dealers and nurseries to recommend biological products (fertilizers, pesticides) directly within the treatment plan, earning a commission on each conversion (Affiliate).

## 2. Development Roadmap

- **Phase 1 (Month 1 – Month 2):** Complete the UI/UX, bootstrap the monorepo structure, design the database and basic APIs with NestJS.
- **Phase 2 (Month 3):** Integrate the AI model (Computer Vision), handle disease-recognition logic, and return treatment plans. Apply Redis caching to optimize speed.
- **Phase 3 (Month 4):** Test on real devices (iOS/Android), package with Docker, deploy the system to GCP/Vercel, and prepare the pitching demo.
